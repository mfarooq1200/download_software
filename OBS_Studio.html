OBS Studio

Free and Open-Source Screen Recording and Live Streaming Software

What Is OBS Studio?

OBS Studio, also known as Open Broadcaster Software Studio, is a free and open-source application used for screen recording, video production, and live streaming. It is popular among content creators, gamers, educators, and professionals who want to record or broadcast their screens.

OBS Studio allows users to combine different video and audio sources into scenes, making it useful for tutorials, gameplay videos, online presentations, podcasts, and live broadcasts.

Key Features of OBS Studio

  • Screen Recording: Record your desktop, applications, presentations, and other on-screen activities.
  • Live Streaming: Broadcast content to supported streaming platforms using the appropriate service settings.
  • Multiple Scenes: Create different scenes and switch between them during recording or a live stream.
  • Audio Mixing: Control microphone and desktop audio with individual volume controls and filters.
  • Webcam Support: Add a webcam to recordings and streams for face-camera videos and presentations.
  • Customizable Layout: Arrange video sources, images, text, windows, and other elements to create your preferred layout.
  • Recording Settings: Customize resolution, frame rate, encoding, and output settings according to your computer's capabilities.
  • Plugins: Extend OBS Studio's functionality with compatible third-party plugins.

Who Can Use OBS Studio?

OBS Studio is suitable for gamers, YouTubers, teachers, online trainers, software developers, podcasters, and businesses. It can be used to create tutorials, record software demonstrations, stream gameplay, and produce professional-looking video content.

System Requirements

OBS Studio supports compatible versions of Windows, macOS, and Linux. The performance you get depends on your operating system, processor, graphics hardware, available memory, recording resolution, and streaming settings.

For smoother recording and streaming, use a computer that meets the requirements for your operating system and has enough processing power for your selected settings.
